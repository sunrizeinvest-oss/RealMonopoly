# Template 05 — Out-of-Province Investor (Diversification Pitch)

**Use for:** `seg3_other_market_investors.csv`
**Channel:** Email
**Goal:** Position Edmonton as the smart geographic diversification away from Toronto / Vancouver / USA

---

## Subject lines (A/B test)
- A: `Edmonton multifamily — diversification play for {{home_market}} investors`
- B: `{{first_name}}, why I moved capital from {{home_market}} to Edmonton`
- C: `MF cap rates 5.5-6.5% — Edmonton vs {{home_market}}`

---

## Body

Hi {{first_name}},

Sunni here — I run Sunrize Investments out of Edmonton. We acquire multifamily and infill in the 5-25 unit range, currently 4 active projects.

Reaching out because most of the investors I work with are spreading capital across markets to manage geographic risk. {{home_market}} investors in particular are increasingly looking at Alberta for diversification — the cap rate spread is meaningful (5.5-6.5% in Edmonton vs 3.5-4.5% in {{home_market}}), Alberta's tax structure is friendlier, and demographics are inbound (~50K net interprovincial migration to AB last year).

We typically structure deals as:
- LP equity, 8% pref, 70/30 split above
- 3-5 year hold, value-add through capex + rent normalization
- IRR target 14-18%

Not pitching a specific deal today — looking to know if Edmonton MF is something you'd want on your radar for 2026. 20-min call to see if there's a fit?

Calendly: [INSERT_LINK]

Best,
Sunni Yaremchuk

## Notes
- Replace `{{home_market}}` with Toronto / Vancouver / USA based on row's `markets` column
- The cap rate numbers above are illustrative — sanity-check current Edmonton MF cap rates before sending
- Diversification angle works much better than "look at this deal" for cold out-of-market contacts
- For USA investors, add: "We can structure through a Canadian holdco — your accountant will need a CA cross-border specialist, happy to refer one"
