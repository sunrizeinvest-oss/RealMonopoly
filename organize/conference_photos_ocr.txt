=== [12/41] IMG_4711.HEIC ===
Financing
• Are you assuming the loan? If so, is there a fee?
• Term of the loan
• Interest rate
• Amortization
• Interest only vs P&l
• Financing/lender fees (vary, from ½-3% or more)
CMHC - up to 95% LTV, 40-year Amortization
Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac - up to 80% LTV, 30-year Amortization
CPI CAPITAL

=== [13/41] IMG_4712.HEIC ===
Renovation and Stabilization
• Cost to renovate per unit
• Cost to renovate exterior per unit
How long will it take to complete the renovations?
How will occupancy be impacted by the renovations?
Be conservative with your timeline - issues always come up!
Get real quotes and use real numbers - don't use general rules of thumb.
CPI CAPITAL

=== [14/41] IMG_4713.HEIC ===
Growth Assumptions
You must project future values for:
• Annual rent increase - be conservative, generally don't go over 2-3%
• Annual expense increase - keep pace with inflation
• Property tax increase
• Insurance increase
• Vacancy rate - use a value worse than today (go 5% to 10% lower)
• Loss to lease - 2-3% OR project your proforma lower than comps
• Concessions - Depending on the market and economy
• Bad Debt (Collection Loss) - 0.5-3%
CPI CAPITAL

=== [22/41] IMG_4774.HEIC ===
I've found having a 2-step lead system is the best balance between quality and
quantity.
Step 1 - Ask the lead for basic contact information in exchange for access to your
lead magnet AND include around 5-6 application questions.
E.g.: How did you hear about me? How much capital are you looking to place? Are
you an accredited investor?
This will give you enough info to discard the fake and trash leads that will just suck
up your time.

=== [23/41] IMG_4775.HEIC ===
You will want to prepare a standard set of questions to ask every potential
investor.
Examples:
• Tell me about your other investments... what do you like, what doesn't work for
• What got you interested in apartments?
• What criteria do you use to determine whether or not you'll invest in a deal?
• If I presented you a deal today, how much might you possibly invest?
• Are you liquid right now?

=== [24/41] IMG_4776.HEIC ===

Be prepared to answer questions like:
• What is your track record?
• How many deals have you taken full cycle?
• How do you pick your deals?
• Have any of your deals lost money?
