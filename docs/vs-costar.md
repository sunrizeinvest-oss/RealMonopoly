## Where each competitor crushes us

### CoStar wins decisively on:
- **US coverage.** They have 30+ years of US data infrastructure. We have zero US data live today.
- **Submarket reports.** Their analyst team writes quarterly market reports. We have AI Reads but no human-written submarket research.
- **Brand trust at the institutional level.** REITs, pension funds, big PE firms ALL use CoStar. That's a moat built over decades.
- **Ownership history + transaction archive.** Decades of sale transactions, owner contact info, broker activity logs.

### Altus wins decisively on:
- **Canadian institutional comps.** Altus is the gold standard for Canadian commercial transaction data. Their archive goes back further than we ever will.
- **Cross-asset coverage.** Office, retail, industrial, hospitality — they cover all asset classes with depth.
- **Appraiser network.** Altus IS the Canadian appraisal data source. Banks and pension funds rely on them.

### Reonomy wins decisively on:
- **US off-market prospecting.** Their core product is "find off-market owners + their contact info" — that's an entirely different product than us.
- **CRM-style workflow.** Lead lists, owner reachout, activity tracking. We don't do that.

## The "Why doesn't CoStar just do this?" answer

Three reasons, in order of importance:

### 1. They've shown 10 years of zero interest in <$5K customers.

CoStar's sales motion is built for enterprise. Their salespeople work on commission against six-figure annual contracts. Asking them to onboard a $99/mo customer is asking them to do uneconomic work. Their company structure literally cannot serve this segment without rebuilding the org chart.

### 2. Cannibalization risk.

If CoStar shipped a $99/mo self-serve tier, every existing $5K-$50K customer would ask "why am I paying 50x?" The marginal revenue from down-market would be negative.

### 3. Speed disadvantage.

CoStar moves on 18-24 month product cycles (enterprise software cadence). We ship features overnight. By the time CoStar attempts a retail tier, we'll have:
- Real Repliers MLS comps via $249/mo subscription
- US coverage via RentCast
- Off-market sourcing layer
- A 1,000+ paying-user base with retention data

The window where they could catch us is ~36 months. After that, our retail brand + retail UX moat is real.
