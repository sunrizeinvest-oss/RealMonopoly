# Demo Loom Scripts

Three scripts, three audiences, one recording each. Record once, send 50x/week, watch conversions roll in.

**Setup before recording any of these:**
1. Open `https://www.realdealestate.app/` in a clean Chrome window (incognito so no auth banner)
2. Hide your bookmarks bar (`⌘ + Shift + B`)
3. Have `docs/sample-ic-memo.pdf` open in another tab (you'll drop the file in for the LTL demo)
4. Open Loom (or QuickTime if you don't have Loom)
5. Record at 1080p, voice-over on, face camera small bottom-right

## Recording tips

### Voice
- Speak ~10% slower than your natural pace. Demos always feel rushed to viewers.
- Lower your voice 1 octave below your phone-call voice. Sounds more confident.
- Pause for 1 second between major moves. Gives the viewer time to catch up.

### Cursor
- Hide cursor when not interacting (Loom: settings → hide cursor → toggle on)
- Use the spotlight feature when you need to point — don't move the cursor around restlessly

### Editing
- Cut the dead air at the beginning AND end
- Add captions (Loom auto-generates; verify the LTL numbers)
- Add your logo + name as an overlay in the bottom-right after the first 5 seconds

### Length discipline
- 30 seconds = LinkedIn DM
- 60 seconds = Cold email to broker
- 90 seconds = Warm intro / investor

If your version runs over by more than 10 seconds, find a cut. Brevity converts.

## Where to send the Loom

### Channels ranked by conversion

| Channel | Target volume | Expected response | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn DM** to commercial brokers | 50/week | 15-20% reply rate | 2-5% to paid demo |
| **Warm intros** to investors | 5/week | 80% reply rate | 30% to call |
| **Cold email** to known multifamily syndicators | 20/week | 8-12% reply rate | 3-5% to paid demo |
| **LinkedIn post** with embedded Loom | 1/week | Algo-dependent | Volume play |
| **CCIM Slack / forum** | as topic emerges | High signal | High conversion when relevant |

### What NOT to do

- Don't paste the Loom in cold-DM to people who haven't engaged with you on LinkedIn first (always like / comment 1-2 posts before DMing)
- Don't send to your existing network all at once — drip it over 2 weeks
- Don't claim "real-time MLS data" until Repliers is paid — your honest pitch is stronger
- Don't follow up more than once. If they ignore the Loom, move on.
