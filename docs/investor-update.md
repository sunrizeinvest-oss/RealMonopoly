# Monthly Investor Update — Template + Playbook

The single most underused tactic in pre-seed fundraising. Founders who send monthly updates raise **2-3x faster** than those who don't. Every "no" today is a "yes" at seed if you keep them on the list.

This doc gives you the exact template + cadence + send list logic.

---

## Why monthly updates work

| Without monthly updates | With monthly updates |
|---|---|
| "No" investors disappear forever | "No" investors become warm leads in 6-12 months |
| You re-pitch from scratch at seed | You pitch a relationship that's been tracked |
| Cold outreach is harder | Investors expect your update — they're pre-sold |
| Network shrinks every month | Network compounds every month |

The math: even a **5% conversion rate** of "no's" to "yes's" at seed adds 1-3 investors per round. That's often the difference between a closed round and a stalled one.

---

## Who to send it to

### Include
- ✅ Every investor you've met (yes, no, or undecided)
- ✅ Anyone who's made a warm intro for you
- ✅ Strategic advisors (founder mentors, lawyers, accountants)
- ✅ Other founders in your stage cohort (compound network effects)

### Exclude
- ❌ Investors you specifically don't want money from (rare but happens)
- ❌ Anyone who explicitly asked to be removed

### How to build the list
- Pull from your CRM (`RizeAI_Investor_CRM.csv` on Desktop)
- Filter status: `meeting1` + `meeting2` + `pass` + `funded` + `dormant`
- That's your monthly update audience

Target list size: **20-50 names by month 3**, **75-150 by month 6**.

## Cadence

| Date | Action |
|---|---|
| 1st of every month | Draft the update (block 90 min) |
| 2nd of every month | Send (8-9am sender's local time) |
| Throughout the month | Drop interesting wins into a "next update" notes file |

Never miss a month. Even if it's "this month was quiet — here's why and what's next."

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ **Don't send to a list of 200+ people** — feels mass-produced. Cap at ~150.
- ❌ **Don't include any specific customer names** without asking first.
- ❌ **Don't share confidential cap-table info** in plain text — bad opsec.
- ❌ **Don't bury bad news** — investors respect candor; they'll find out anyway.
- ❌ **Don't ask for money in every update** — alternate asks (intros / advice / feedback).
- ❌ **Don't make the email longer than 3 minutes to read** — they have 10 other emails to triage.
- ❌ **Don't send from a noreply@ address** — must be from your real address so they can reply.

---

## What TO include over time

Build these up naturally — don't try to force them into the first update:

- **Real customer quotes** ("This saved me 3 hours on my last underwriting" — by month 3)
- **Specific deal outcomes** ("Customer X used the LTL parser to win a 24-unit at $187K under ask" — by month 4)
- **Aggregate platform stats** (deals analyzed, total LTL surfaced, etc. — by month 6)
- **Hiring announcements** (when you make your first hire — celebrated, not buried)
- **Press / media mentions** (when they happen)
- **Conferences you'll be at** (CCIM Connect, RECONX, Buildex — invite investors to meet)

---

## Tracking responses

After each update goes out, log every reply in your CRM. The pattern that matters:

| Reply type | What it means | Action |
|---|---|---|
| "Great update — let me know how I can help" | Engaged but no specific offer | Reply with the specific ask again |
| Specific intro offered | High value | Send the intro request within 24h |
| "Want to talk about a follow-on at seed" | Hot lead | Set up the call within a week |
| "Pass — but keep me posted" | Still in the funnel | Don't ask anything; keep them on the list |
| No reply 3 months running | Cold | Personalize the next update to them specifically |

---

## The "send list" lifecycle

Month 1: 15-20 names
Month 3: 30-45 names
Month 6: 75-100 names
Month 12: 100-150 names

Investors share your updates internally — your seed round will close with people you've never directly emailed but who've been forwarded 3-6 of your updates.

## Bottom line

This is **30-60 minutes per month** of work. It's the single highest-ROI activity you'll do as a pre-seed founder, second only to actually shipping product and talking to customers.

Block it on your calendar. The 2nd of every month. Forever. Until you're funded for Series A.
